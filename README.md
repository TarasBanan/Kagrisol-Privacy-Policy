Privacy Policy for KAGPositioning

Effective date: 6 June 2026
Last updated: 6 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kagrisol handles information in connection with the KAGPositioning mobile application (the "App"). By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

We have designed KAGPositioning to keep your data on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not sell your personal data.
1. Information we process

a) Location data.
The App's core purpose is positioning, so it accesses your device's GPS/location, including latitude and longitude, altitude, accuracy, speed, heading/compass direction, and satellite/signal information. Location is processed on your device to show your position, save points, record tracks, calculate distance and area, and display maps.

b) Saved content you create.
Bookmarks/saved points, zones, recorded tracks, photos with coordinate stamps, and your app settings are stored locally on your device. Photos you capture through the App may be saved to your device's gallery/storage.

c) Camera and photos.
If you use the photo-stamp feature, the App accesses the camera to take a picture and adds coordinate/altitude/timestamp text to it. These photos are stored on your device.

d) Device and technical data.
To provide map, search, weather, and elevation features, your device sends standard network requests (which include your IP address and the coordinates needed for the request) to the third-party services listed in Section 3.

We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the App does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
2. How we use information

We use the information solely to:

    determine and display your current location;
    save, display, and manage your points, zones, and tracks;
    calculate distances, areas, and navigation guidance;
    show maps and look up addresses, places, weather, and elevation;
    notify you when you enter a configured zone (geofence);
    let you export your data (e.g. GPX/KML files) when you choose to.

3. Third-party services

To provide certain features, the App sends the minimum necessary data (such as coordinates or a search query, together with your IP address) to the following services. We do not control these providers; their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

    OpenStreetMap / Nominatim (address search and reverse geocoding) — https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
    OpenStreetMap tile servers / OpenTopoMap (map tiles) — https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy / https://opentopomap.org/about
    Overpass API (points of interest) — https://overpass-api.de
    Open-Meteo (weather forecast and elevation) — https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
    Google Play (app distribution and update checks) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you configure a custom map style provider, requests will also be sent to that provider under its own terms.
4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except as needed to operate the features described in Section 3, or when required by law. Data exported by you (e.g. GPX/KML files) is shared only by your own action, to destinations you select.
5. Data storage, retention, and deletion

Your bookmarks, zones, tracks, settings, and stamped photos remain on your device for as long as you keep them or until you delete them within the App. You can:

    delete individual saved points, zones, or tracks in the App;
    delete photos through your device gallery;
    remove all App data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings.

We do not maintain our own servers storing your personal data.
6. Permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions, each used only for the related feature:

    Location (fine/approximate): to determine and display your position.
    Camera: to take coordinate-stamped photos.
    Storage / photos: to save and display photos.
    Notifications: to alert you about zone entry events.
    Internet / network state: to load maps and look up addresses, weather, and elevation.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings; some features may stop working as a result.
7. Children's privacy

KAGPositioning is a general-purpose positioning tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.
8. Security

We rely on your device's built-in security and operating-system protections to safeguard locally stored data. Network requests to third-party services use encrypted (HTTPS) connections where supported. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Because the App stores your data locally on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly by viewing, editing, or deleting data in the App, or by uninstalling it. For any other requests, contact us using the details below.
10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
